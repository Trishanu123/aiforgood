# OptiBuild AI — 2-minute demo script

Goal: show that fragmented building, utility, incentive, and financial data becomes one preliminary retrofit report.

1. Open the home page. Point to the tagline and the workflow from Building → Action plan. Say: this is a screening tool, not an audit.
2. Click **Try Demo**. No login. Watch the live pipeline (building, utility, baseline, opportunities, incentives, financials).
3. On the report, call out the four hero numbers: estimated annual energy cost, potential annual savings (with range), modeled incentives, best preliminary payback.
4. Scroll recommended solutions. Open **View calculation** on lighting or HVAC to show transparent arithmetic.
5. Sort the comparison table. Mention priority is not ROI-only.
6. Show charts (cost before/after, savings by technology, 10-year cash position).
7. Open an incentive card. Emphasize official source, “see program requirements,” and eligibility must be confirmed.
8. Close on next steps, assumptions, sources, and the disclaimer.

If asked about AI: bills can be uploaded for structured extraction; explanations are grounded in calculated numbers. Missing keys still run the demo with labeled fallbacks.
